---
name: research
description: Decompose a question, search narrowly, cite what was found, and label inference as inference.
kind: method
---
1. **Restate the question** in one line, including what a good answer would let the reader do.
2. **Decompose** it into at most three sub-questions that can each be answered by evidence.
3. **Search narrowly.** One query per sub-question, aimed at primary sources. Do not search in a
   loop hoping for a different answer.
4. **Record provenance.** For every claim, name the source and label it **seen** (you read it),
   **inferred** (you reasoned from what you read) or **unknown**. Never present inference as seen.
5. **Answer the question first**, then the evidence, then what is still unknown. A research answer
   that hides its uncertainty is not finished.
